08FAQ

A few things
worth knowing.

The essentials of locks, vesting and public proof.

Can I use Damkeeper with real tokens today?

This page is an interactive product preview. The schedules and positions use example data. Live contract addresses and deployment details have not been published here yet.

When can locked tokens be withdrawn?

Only after the unlock date, and only by the withdrawal wallet named when the lock was created. Before that date the contract rejects every withdrawal.

How does vesting release tokens?

Tokens vest in a straight line from the start date to the end date. If the schedule has a cliff, nothing is claimable before it. The release curve is shown before you sign, including how much becomes available at the cliff.

How does the Token Burn feature work?

Damkeeper provides verifiable onchain token destruction on Robinhood Chain. For tokens implementing ERC20Burnable, tokens are permanently destroyed, reducing the contract's totalSupply. For standard ERC-20 tokens, tokens are sent to the provably unspendable 0x000...dEaD sink. Each burn produces a public cryptographic proof card with tx receipt and supply contraction metrics.

Are vested tokens sent automatically?

No. The beneficiary claims them. Vested tokens stay in the contract until claimed, and only the beneficiary can claim.

Which tokens are supported?

Standard ERC-20 tokens on Robinhood Chain. Support for tokens with transfer fees, rebasing or other custom behavior will be documented before launch.

What if the app or indexer is down?

Positions are designed to be recorded in smart contracts. Published contract addresses will let users inspect their terms directly on the explorer, independently of the app.

How do I verify a position?

Open its proof page. It lists the terms, token address, creator, beneficiary, history and contract address, each linked to Blockscout. No wallet connection needed.
